Welcome to the Study

Thank you for participating in this research!

Overview of the Experiment

This study aims to evaluate the engagement of participants when interacting with chatbots designed to simulate conversations. Specifically, we'll explore your experience when using different conversation prompts.

What to Expect

  • Duration: The experiment will take approximately 30 minutes.
  • Tasks: You will have two, five minute, conversations with different chatbots. During each conversation, you'll be asked to recall and discuss either a mildly positive or mildly challenging personal memory.
  • Questionnaires: Basic demographics (age/gender/education level) will be collected prior to chatbot interactions. Before and after each chat, you'll complete short questionnaires about your feelings and experiences during the interactions.

Important Ethical Considerations

  • Not a therapy session: This study is not therapeutic and should not be treated as a replacement for professional mental health support.
  • Confidentiality and anonymity: While chatting with the bots, please ensure you do not share anything you wouldn't be comfortable being reviewed.
  • Participant safety monitoring: The chat will be monitored live by the researcher during the study.

Your Participation

Participation is entirely voluntary. You can pause or withdraw from the experiment at any time without needing to give a reason.

If you have questions or concerns about the study, please feel free to ask the researcher at any point.
