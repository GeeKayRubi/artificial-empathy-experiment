Study Title:

Artificial Empathy: Evaluating Emotional Engagement with Therapeutic Chatbots

Researcher:

Georgia Rubidge (Supervised by Dr. Szymkowiak & Dr. Shepherd)

What was this study about?

This study explored how different chatbot interaction styles influence your emotional experience. The aim is to improve the design of therapeutic chatbots for more effective and emotionally supportive conversations.

Why did we ask about personal memories?

Reflecting on personal experiences helps activate real emotional responses, which allows us to better assess the emotional impact of the chatbot’s style and tone.
