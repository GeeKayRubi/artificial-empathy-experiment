Brief reading on AI Chatbots:

"Generative AI can be deceptively realistic in its conversational tone, but it does not reason or think like a human. It predicts each word by analyzing millions of data points to determine what comes next. Essentially, LLMs are extraordinarily advanced autocomplete systems, similar to the predictive text feature on your smartphone but trained to function at a near-human level of sophistication. They do not ‘understand’ text in the human sense; they simply predict what might come next based on patterns in the data." (Adapted from Artigliere & Losey, 2024)
